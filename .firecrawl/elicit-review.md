**how it works**

## Elicit helps accelerate every step of a systematic review

Step 1

### Refine your protocol

Choosing the right review protocol is half the battle. Use Elicit to refine research questions and search strategies.

![Elicit interface showing a research question about theanine and cognitive function with AI-suggested refinement tags.](https://framerusercontent.com/images/aJOcAqZ7597fxqgWwSJAb9czl8.png?width=1480&height=546)

Step 2

### Gather every source

Combine searches across PubMed, ClinicalTrials.gov, Elicit’s corpus of 138 million papers, or import from other databases.

Run reproducible keyword searches or use Elicit’s semantic search to ensure you don’t miss any relevant papers.

![Elicit search results showing top papers with relevance and citation counts.](https://framerusercontent.com/images/nnzEDcGFFGYLVyBDRp4Y2kLMwU.png?width=990&height=620)

Step 3

### Screen each paper

Screen up to 40,000 papers in real-time.

Elicit autosuggests screening criteria and recommendations. You can agree, edit, or add your own.

Every decision is PRISMA-auditable with exclusion reasons, per-criterion scores, and supporting quotes. Dual review pairs two humans with AI assistance, or uses AI as the second reviewer

![Elicit interface for title and abstract screening showing an AI-generated inclusion recommendation with detailed rationale](https://framerusercontent.com/images/dOVrsqwkns3q7Mj4jDskPTQw9A.png?width=884&height=480)

Step 4

### Extract data

Data extraction is one of Elicit's superpowers. Elicit has the most accurate data extraction of any AI product, including from tables and figures, where most clinical outcomes actually live.

We can extract both quantitative and qualitative data. Every extraction is supported by quotes or figures from the source.

Iterate with our AI-suggested data extraction templates, or create your own.

![Elicit interface extracting quantitative data, such as safety thresholds, from multiple research papers into a summary table](https://framerusercontent.com/images/tle7ZXJTSkUgyf7z0wXfBAzXGU.png?width=590&height=430)

Step 5

### Synthesize the evidence

Elicit generates reports synthesizing across up to 200 papers, with each claim backed by sentence-level citations from the sources.

Use one of our built-in templates or customize one for your organization.

All reports contain a methods section with PRISMA flow diagrams, tables of paper summaries, and search strategies.

![Elicit-generated research report summarizing biological pathways with sentence-level citations from original papers.](https://framerusercontent.com/images/zgr1l2GOBB1MFrX4HaGAndXeH1Q.png?width=2970&height=1902)

## Can AI take systematic reviews   to the next level?

Conforming to today's guidelines is necessary. But some of those guidelines were designed around the constraints of doing work manually. How can AI enable even more impactful evidence synthesis?

### Living reviews

Combine Elicit Systematic Reviews and Alerts to create living reviews - easily updating systematic reviews with new information every year, or as needed

### Transparency for free

Once you finish your review, you can share a link to the entire process and intermediate results with anyone, even someone who does not have an Elicit account. The recipient can review every step you took.

### Iterative protocol design

It's hard to define your review protocol without knowing what research is or is not out there.

An automated approach helps you iterate and test different criteria quickly before committing to one.

### Resuable data

The real treasure is the papers we reviewed along the way.

Doing your reviews in Elicit makes it easier to reuse some of the papers you painstakingly reviewed in other projects.
