Weeknight cooking in a small kitchen. Staples always in stock: olive oil, salt, pepper, chilli flakes, garlic, onions, lemons, eggs, butter, yoghurt, rice, pasta, canned tomatoes, chickpeas, soy sauce, honey, vinegar. Restock rice and pasta on Sundays.

Go-to meals: tomato pasta (~20 min), egg fried rice with day-old rice, chickpea stew with yoghurt, sheet-pan chicken thighs with potatoes (~40 min oven). Weekdays stick to known recipes; experiments on Saturday. When tired, one protein + one carb + one green — or just pasta and eggs.

Sunday prep: wash salad leaves, cook rice for later fried rice, chop onions for two nights, check eggs/yoghurt/tomatoes. No five identical meal-prep boxes. Freeze tomato sauce in small tubs for busy weeks.

Mistakes: crowding the pan steams chicken; burnt garlic from adding too early; unsalted pasta water; reheating rice without a splash of water; buying soft herbs for one recipe that wilt unused.

Shopping: short list, protein for 2–3 nights, one green, one fruit, restock staples only when nearly empty. Chicken thighs over breast. Skip new sauces unless two meals will use them.
