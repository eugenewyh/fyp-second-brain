# Weeknight meals

Favourites that fit a tired evening:

1. **Tomato pasta** — garlic in oil, canned tomatoes, chilli flakes, pasta water to loosen. Done in ~20 minutes.
2. **Egg fried rice** — cold leftover rice, eggs, soy sauce, spring onion. Best when the rice is day-old.
3. **Chickpea stew** — onion, garlic, canned tomatoes, chickpeas, cumin. Serve with yoghurt and bread.
4. **Sheet-pan chicken** — chicken thighs, potatoes, olive oil, salt. 40 minutes in the oven while I do other things.

I cook **one protein + one carb + one green** when I have energy. When I do not, pasta with eggs is enough.

I avoid new recipes on weekdays. Experiments stay on Saturdays.
