# Shopping rules

I shop with a short list, not a mood:

- Protein for 2–3 nights
- One green vegetable
- One fruit for snacks
- Restock staples only when the jar is near empty

I buy **loose vegetables** for the next few days, not giant bags that rot in the crisper.

Market tip: chicken thighs over breast for weeknights. They stay juicy if I overcook a little, and they are cheaper.

I skip “new sauce of the week” unless I already know two meals that will use it. Fancy bottles become fridge clutter.
