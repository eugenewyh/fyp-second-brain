# Prep habits

Sunday prep that actually helps:

1. Wash and dry salad leaves; store in a box with a paper towel.
2. Cook a pot of rice for fried rice later in the week.
3. Chop onions for two nights and keep them sealed in the fridge.
4. Check that eggs, yoghurt, and canned tomatoes are in stock.

I do **not** full meal-prep five identical boxes. They bore me by Wednesday and I waste food.

Before a busy week, I freeze one batch of tomato sauce in small tubs. Thawing that is faster than starting from a can when I am late.
