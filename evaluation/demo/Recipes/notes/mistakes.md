# Mistakes I keep making

**Crowding the pan.** Too much chicken at once steams instead of browning. Cook in two batches even when I am impatient.

**Adding garlic too early.** Burnt garlic makes the whole dish bitter. Soften onion first, then garlic for the last minute.

**Skipping salt until the end.** Under-seasoned pasta water and unsalted sauces taste flat no matter how much cheese I add later.

**Reheating rice badly.** Cold blocks in the microwave stay hard. Splash in a spoon of water, cover, then heat.

**Buying herbs “for one recipe.”** Soft herbs wilt before I use them again. I buy parsley or coriander only when two meals need them the same week.
