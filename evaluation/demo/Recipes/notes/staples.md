# Pantry staples

I keep a short list so weeknight cooking does not need a special shop:

- Olive oil, salt, black pepper, chilli flakes
- Garlic, onions, lemons
- Eggs, butter, plain yoghurt
- Rice, pasta, canned tomatoes, chickpeas
- Soy sauce, honey, vinegar

If these are in the kitchen, I can make something edible in under 40 minutes.

I restock rice and pasta on Sundays. Running out mid-week is how I end up ordering takeaway.
