# Water, puck prep, and this week’s dial-in

Notes from practice — file these into Coffee memory.

## Water

Filtered water around **100–150 ppm** hardness works best for me.
Soft water under-extracts and tastes flat. Very hard water scales the machine and mutes sweetness.
I heat the machine **15–20 minutes** before the first shot so the group is fully hot.

## Puck prep

1. Dose into the basket.
2. Distribute with a **WDT** (thin needles).
3. Level, then tamp straight down — about **15–20 kg**, firm and level, not a workout.

If the shot blondes early on one side, that’s channeling. Re-WDT and check the tamp isn’t tilted.

## Milk steaming reminder

Tip barely under the surface for 2–3 seconds to stretch, then bury and spin to **60–65°C**.
Wipe and purge the wand every time — otherwise the next flat white smells like old milk.
