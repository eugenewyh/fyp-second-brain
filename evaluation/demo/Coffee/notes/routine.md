# Morning routine

Weekday default:

1. Heat the machine while I grind 18 g.
2. WDT, tamp level, pull a double aiming for ~36 g out in 25–30 seconds.
3. Taste before adding water or milk. If it is harsh, I fix the next shot — I do not “save” a bad one with sugar.

If the shot is too sour, I grind finer or lengthen the ratio slightly. If it is too bitter or dry, I grind coarser or stop a little earlier.

Weekday drinks: straight double espresso, or a small americano if I want something to sip.

Weekend treat: flat white. Oat milk when I want something lighter; dairy when I want classic sweetness.

I rinse the group head and wipe the steam wand every time. Skipping that is how milk smells show up in the next espresso.
