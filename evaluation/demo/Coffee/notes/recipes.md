# Recipes that work for me

## Everyday espresso

- Dose: 18 g in
- Yield: about 36 g out (1:2)
- Time: roughly 25–30 seconds from pump start
- Change **grind one notch at a time**. Only change dose if taste is still wrong after three shots.

## Small americano

- Same double espresso
- Add 90–120 ml hot water after the shot (not before), so I can taste the espresso first

## Flat white

- Double espresso in a small cup
- Steam milk to **microfoam like wet paint**, not big bubbles
- Stop around **60–65°C** — hotter milk tastes cooked and loses sweetness
- Pour to about 150–180 ml total drink

## “Guest” darker cup

If someone wants a stronger bitter profile, I use a slightly darker bean and a touch longer ratio — I do not ruin my weekday light-medium bag trying to fake it.
