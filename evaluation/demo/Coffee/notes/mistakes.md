# Mistakes I keep not repeating

Buying a nicer machine before fixing the grinder wasted money. Uneven grounds beat any machine upgrade.

Tamping harder does not fix a bad grind. It just makes flow weird. Level and even distribution matter more than force.

Pulling shot after shot without cleaning the basket left old fines that made everything bitter.

Using unfiltered hard tap water left scale and muted flavour. Filtered water helped more than a new basket.

Scalding milk past ~70°C made lattes taste flat. Sweetness lives in the cooler range.

- Would a naked portafilter help me spot channeling faster?
- Is a single-dose grinder worth it for one person who only pulls two shots a day?
- How much does water mineral content change espresso sweetness on my machine?
