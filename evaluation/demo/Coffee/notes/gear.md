# Gear I actually use

I brew on a compact entry-level espresso machine with a pressurized basket for busy mornings and a non-pressurized basket when I want to practice.

The most important upgrade was a **burr grinder**. A blade grinder made every shot inconsistent — sour and bitter in the same cup. I will not go back.

I keep a cheap digital scale next to the machine. Weighing beans in and liquid out is how I repeat a good shot. Guessing by eye failed me for months.

Accessories that earn their space:

- WDT tool (thin needles) to break up clumps before tamping
- Simple distributor / leveler so the puck is flat
- Soft cloth and backflush tablets for weekly cleaning

What I would buy next (in order): a single-dose burr grinder with less retention, then maybe a naked portafilter so I can see channeling. A bigger dual-boiler machine is last — the grinder gap hurts taste more.
