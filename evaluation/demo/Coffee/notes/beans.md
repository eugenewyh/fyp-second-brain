# Beans and roast

I like **medium or light-medium** roasted single-origin beans for morning espresso. They stay sweeter and show fruit or chocolate notes instead of ash.

Very **dark, oily** beans taste flat on my setup and leave more residue in the basket. I avoid them unless a guest specifically wants a classic dark roast.

I buy small bags (250 g) and aim to finish them within about three weeks of the roast date. Stale beans go dull no matter how careful the recipe is.

Favourite everyday profiles so far:

- Washed Central American — clean chocolate, nutty, easy milk pairing
- Natural Ethiopian — berry sweetness on weekends when I drink it straight

I store beans in an opaque airtight tin at room temperature. Freezing is only for backup bags I will not open for a month.
