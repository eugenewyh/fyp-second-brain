I keep snake plant, golden pothos, and monstera in a small apartment. East window, bright indirect light. Pull pots back from glass in summer so afternoon sun does not scorch leaves. Rotate pots weekly.

Watering: finger test — only when top 2–3 cm is dry. Snake plant every 2–3 weeks. Pothos roughly weekly in warm months. Always empty saucers after 15 minutes. Prefer morning watering. When unsure, wait another day — overwatering kills more of my plants than underwatering.

Mistakes: fixed-calendar watering caused yellow leaves; decorative pots without drainage rotted roots (use nursery pot in cachepot); fertilizing every watering burned tips (monthly in spring/summer only). Do not repot the same day I buy a plant.

Sunday habit: check soil, water who needs it, wipe dusty leaves, snip dead bits. Before travel longer than 5 days, one thorough water and leave them in place — no bathtub soaking.
