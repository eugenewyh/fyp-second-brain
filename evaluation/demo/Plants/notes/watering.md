# Watering

Default habit: check soil with a finger once or twice a week. Water only when the top 2–3 cm feels dry.

What works for me:

- Snake plant: every 2–3 weeks. It hates wet feet.
- Pothos: roughly weekly in warm months; less in winter.
- Monstera: when the top of the pot feels dry — usually every 7–10 days.
- Always empty the saucer after 15 minutes so roots do not sit in water.

I water in the morning so leaves dry during the day. Evening watering left water on leaves overnight and invited spots.

If I am unsure, I wait another day. Overwatering has killed more of my plants than underwatering.
