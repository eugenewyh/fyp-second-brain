# Light

Most of my plants sit near an east window — morning sun, soft afternoon light.

Rules I stick to:

- Bright indirect light for pothos, snake plant, and monstera.
- Direct harsh afternoon sun scorches leaves — I pull pots back ~30 cm from the glass in summer.
- If a plant stretches tall and sparse (leggy), it wants more light, not more water.
- Rotate pots a quarter turn every week so growth stays even.

I do **not** keep plants in the windowless bathroom. That was a failed experiment — everything went pale and soft.
