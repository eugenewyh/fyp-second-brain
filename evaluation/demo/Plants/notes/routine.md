# Weekly habit

Sunday evening, ~10 minutes:

1. Walk past each plant and finger-check soil.
2. Water only the ones that need it; empty saucers.
3. Wipe dusty monstera leaves with a damp cloth.
4. Snip yellow or dead bits so I notice new problems next week.
5. Note anything odd in my phone (e.g. “pothos pale — move closer to window”).

I do **not** fertilize on this walk unless it is a scheduled spring/summer week.

Travel tip: before a trip longer than 5 days, water thoroughly once, then leave plants where they are. No last-minute moves into the bathtub — that made the snake plant sit wet for a week last time.
