# Mistakes

Things I already learned the hard way:

- **Yellow lower leaves + wet soil** → I was watering on a fixed calendar. Switch to the finger test.
- **Bought a plant and repotted the same day** → shock. Now I wait 1–2 weeks before changing pots.
- **Decorative pots without drainage** → roots rotted. Nursery pot inside a cachepot; lift to water, then put back.
- **Misted leaves every day** for humidity theatre. Did almost nothing; a pebble tray under the monstera helped more.
- **Fertilizer every watering** → crispy tips. Now: light feed once a month in spring/summer only. None in winter.

If a plant looks sad, I check light and wetness first — not the garden centre for a new “cure” product.
